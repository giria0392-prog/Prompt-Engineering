About Me

I'm a second-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at JECRC University, Jaipur, specializing in AI & Data Science.

Most of what I know, I've picked up by building things — trying an idea, running into a wall, and figuring out why it broke. That's shaped how I approach software development, web development, and artificial intelligence: less about memorizing theory, more about seeing what actually holds up when you build with it.

Outside of coursework, I'm drawn to startups and entrepreneurship — thinking about real problems people run into and whether a piece of software could genuinely make that easier. I'm still early in this journey, and I'm working on my technical depth and my ability to communicate ideas clearly, one project at a time.
